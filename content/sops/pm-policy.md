# Cadence

Aquatic life-support systems follow a **quarterly** preventive maintenance cadence. Each PM includes:

- Filter media inspection and replacement.
- Shaft seal visual inspection.
- Pump bearing temperature check.
- Sensor calibration against reference standards.
- Backup pump exercise cycle (10 minute run).

# Shaft-seal wear pattern

Shaft seals on the LSS-200 series are consumable. Documented mean time between replacements is **9 months** under nominal duty. Signs of accelerating wear:

- Weep at the seal housing during PM.
- Pump pressure oscillation of ±3 psi around nominal.
- Progressive decline in dissolved oxygen readings when otherwise nominal.

A weep observed at a PM should trigger a follow-up inspection **within 60 days** and a scheduled replacement. Progression from weep to drip requires immediate shaft-seal replacement.

# PM compliance

Missed PMs are logged as compliance events in the audit trail. Facilities Manager is responsible for scheduling; on-call maintenance is responsible for execution.
