# Purpose

Defines the immediate response when the aquatic life-support system (LSS-204) serving the River's Edge habitat exhibits an out-of-band dissolved-oxygen (DO), pump-pressure, or water-temperature reading.

# Thresholds

The following thresholds are species-appropriate for the current River's Edge inhabitants:

- **Dissolved oxygen**: 6.0 mg/L minimum (nominal 8.5 mg/L).
- **Pump pressure**: 22–32 psi (nominal 27 psi).
- **Water temperature**: 20–26 °C (nominal 23 °C).

Any reading crossing these bands escalates per the response ladder below.

# Response ladder

## Step 1 — Engage backup pump LSS-204B
If **either** primary pump pressure drops below 22 psi **or** dissolved oxygen drops below 6.0 mg/L, engage the backup pump LSS-204B via the wall-mounted transfer switch in the equipment corridor. Confirm rotation on the local panel within 60 seconds. Backup engagement alone typically restores DO within 4–6 minutes.

## Step 2 — Manual O₂ supplementation
If dissolved oxygen remains below 6.0 mg/L two minutes after backup engagement, begin manual oxygen supplementation using the portable diffuser stored in the equipment room (Locker A-3). Continue until DO reaches 7.5 mg/L for at least two consecutive readings.

## Step 3 — Notify per policy
Notify the on-duty aquatics curator immediately for any critical alert. Notify the Facilities Manager and General Curator within 15 minutes of any welfare-affecting event. See the Escalation Policy (SOP-ESC-002) for the full notification matrix.

## Step 4 — Document and open incident
Open an incident in the operational workspace with the alert reference, actions taken, and outcome. Attach any relevant sensor timeline. Incident closure requires two consecutive nominal readings for all three metrics.

# Contra-indications

- Do **not** cycle the primary pump repeatedly if pressure is unstable — this masks bearing or shaft-seal failure. Escalate to maintenance and rely on the backup pump.
- Do **not** exceed 10 minutes of manual O₂ supplementation without a curator on-site.
- If water temperature exceeds 26 °C in conjunction with a DO drop, treat as a compound event and involve the veterinary team via the escalation policy.
